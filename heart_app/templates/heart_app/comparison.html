Metric guide

Quick definitions for the values shown in the report.

AccuracyProportion of all held-out test predictions that match the true labels.
PrecisionAmong predicted positive cases, the proportion that are truly positive.
Recall (Sensitivity)Among actual positive cases, the proportion correctly identified.
F-score (F1)Harmonic mean of precision and recall.
